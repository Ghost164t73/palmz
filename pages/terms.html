PALMZ Rewards

For every five qualifying shoe pairs purchased while signed in, a customer earns one free shoe. A pair counts after its order has been paid and confirmed; cancelled orders do not count. The free shoe must have a listed price no higher than the highest-priced pair in the qualifying group of five. Reward progress is shown in the customer account. Contact PALMZ to select and arrange the reward.
