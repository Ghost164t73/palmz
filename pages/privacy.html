Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy describes how PALMZ handles personal information when you browse our website, contact us, place an order, sign in, or request a custom shoe. We handle information in line with applicable Nigerian data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Information we collect

  • Orders: name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, items and variants ordered, delivery or pickup details, order notes, and payment status or reference.
  • Customer accounts: Google account name, verified email address, profile image if provided, saved order history, and reward progress.
  • Custom shoe requests: contact details, design notes, and any image you choose to upload.
  • Payment details: Paystack processes card and other payment credentials. PALMZ receives transaction verification details such as the payment reference, amount, and status; we do not receive or store your full card number or security code.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries and custom requests, process and deliver orders, verify payments, maintain order history and rewards, provide customer support, protect the website from misuse, and meet legal or accounting obligations. We do not use order information for unrelated advertising.

Service providers and sharing

We share only the information needed with service providers that help operate the store: Google for account sign-in, Paystack for online payments, MongoDB hosting for account and order records, Resend for transactional emails, and Cloudinary for custom-request image storage. When you choose to order through WhatsApp, you send order details to PALMZ through WhatsApp. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices and may process it in locations outside Nigeria.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect customers or PALMZ, or to investigate suspected fraud or security incidents. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and account security

Signing in uses a necessary, first-party session cookie to keep you signed in. The customer session cookie is HTTP-only and expires after seven days. The site also uses local browser storage for shopping bag contents. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please keep your Google account secure and contact us if you believe your account or order information has been accessed without permission.

How long we keep information

We keep account and order information while needed to provide the service, manage customer support and rewards, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Custom-request images and correspondence are retained as needed to respond to and manage the request. Retention periods can vary according to these purposes and legal requirements.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information, or raise an objection or other privacy request, subject to applicable law and records we are required to retain. You can sign out of your account at any time. To make a privacy request, contact PALMZ through the Contact page and include enough information for us to locate the relevant account or order. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Children and policy updates

The website is intended for people able to place orders and provide valid contact details. Please do not submit another person's personal information or images without permission. We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change; the latest version and update date will appear here.
