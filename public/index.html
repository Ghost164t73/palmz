MADE A LITTLE MORE YOU

Customize your shoe

Tell us what you have in mind. You can attach a reference image, and our team will email you to discuss the design. This is a design request, not an order or payment.

Your reference image and design details will be sent to the PALMZ team by email so they can follow up with you.

What helps us make it right?

Share as much detail as you can. A clear reference image is useful, but it’s optional.

  • Your usual shoe size and fit preference
  • Colors, materials, sole, patterns, and placement of details
  • A target budget or the date you need it by

We’ll review your idea and contact you to discuss availability, timing, and pricing.
